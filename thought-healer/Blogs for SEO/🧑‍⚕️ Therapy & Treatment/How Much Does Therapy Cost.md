# How Much Does Therapy Cost? A Guide to Therapy Fees in India

If you're considering therapy, one of the first practical questions you may have is: **“How much does therapy cost?”**

The answer depends on several factors, including where you live, the type of professional you see, their qualifications and experience, whether the session is online or in person, and the type of therapy you need.

In India, private therapy sessions can range from relatively affordable counselling to several thousand rupees per session. Current listings in Pune, for example, show psychologists with consultation fees ranging from around **₹650 to ₹3,500 or more**, while some platforms describe typical psychologist fees in particular localities as roughly ₹500–₹2,000 per session.

There are also **free government mental-health services** available. India's Tele-MANAS program provides 24/7 tele-mental-health support through **14416** or **1800-89-14416**.

So if cost is stopping you from seeking help, you may have more options than you realize.

# How Much Does Therapy Cost in India?

There is **no single standard therapy fee in India**.

Private therapists and psychologists set their own fees, and the price can vary substantially.

As a practical current reference, online listings in Pune show examples ranging from approximately **₹650 to ₹3,500 per consultation**, depending on the professional and clinic.

That doesn't mean every therapist will charge within that range.

You may find:

- Lower-cost counselling services
    
- Student or trainee counsellors
    
- Community mental-health programs
    
- Therapists charging moderate private fees
    
- Highly experienced specialists charging considerably more
    
- Hospital-based mental-health services
    
- Online therapy options
    
- Government-supported services
    

The important thing is to compare **qualifications, experience, treatment approach, and suitability**, rather than choosing solely according to price.

# Why Does Therapy Cost So Much?

You may wonder why two therapists can charge very different amounts for what appears to be the same service.

Several factors influence the price.

## 1. Qualifications

Different mental-health professionals have different educational backgrounds, training, professional roles, and scopes of practice.

When comparing therapists, look beyond the title and check their actual qualifications and professional credentials.

---

## 2. Experience

Professionals with many years of experience or specialized training may charge more.

For example, someone who has developed expertise in a particular area such as OCD, trauma, couples therapy, or child and adolescent mental health may have higher fees.

Higher cost doesn't automatically mean better treatment, but specialized expertise can be valuable when it matches your needs.

---

## 3. Location

Therapy fees can differ between:

- Large metropolitan cities
    
- Smaller cities
    
- Towns
    
- Rural areas
    

Rent, operating costs, demand, and availability of specialists can all influence pricing.

---

## 4. Type of therapy

Some concerns require specialized treatment.

For example, a therapist providing structured treatment for OCD, trauma, eating disorders, or complex psychological difficulties may have additional training.

The type of treatment can therefore affect the cost.

---

## 5. Session length

A standard session might last around 45–60 minutes, but session length varies.

A longer appointment may cost more.

Always ask how long a session lasts when comparing fees.

---

## 6. Online vs. in-person therapy

Online therapy can sometimes be less expensive than in-person treatment, although this isn't always the case.

Some professionals charge the same fee for online and in-person sessions.

Ask before booking.

# How Much Does Online Therapy Cost?

There isn't one standard price for online therapy in India.

Online sessions may be offered by:

- Independent therapists
    
- Psychology platforms
    
- Hospitals
    
- Mental-health clinics
    
- Counselling services
    
- Telehealth providers
    

The fee can range from relatively inexpensive to several thousand rupees depending on the professional and service.

For example, some therapists listed on platforms such as Practo offer video consultations alongside clinic appointments.

Before booking, check:

- Session price
    
- Session duration
    
- Therapist qualifications
    
- Cancellation policy
    
- Whether follow-up sessions cost the same
    
- Whether online sessions are video or audio
    
- Whether taxes or platform charges apply

# How Much Does a Psychiatrist Cost?

A psychiatrist is a medical doctor who specializes in mental health.

Psychiatrists can assess mental-health conditions and, when appropriate, prescribe medication.

Their consultation fees vary according to:

- Location
    
- Hospital or clinic
    
- Experience
    
- Consultation length
    
- Specialist expertise
    
- Whether follow-up is required
    

A psychiatric consultation is also not necessarily the same thing as a therapy session.

Depending on your needs, you might see:

- A psychologist or therapist for psychotherapy
    
- A psychiatrist for medical assessment and medication
    
- Both
    

A qualified professional can help determine which type of care is appropriate.

# Is Therapy Covered by Health Insurance in India?

Coverage depends on the specific insurance policy.

Mental-health coverage has expanded under India's insurance framework, but **the exact benefits, limits, exclusions, provider requirements, and reimbursement process vary by policy**.

Don't assume that every therapy session will automatically be reimbursed.

Before starting treatment, contact your insurer and ask:

- Is outpatient mental-health treatment covered?
    
- Are psychologist consultations covered?
    
- Are psychiatrist consultations covered?
    
- Is psychotherapy covered?
    
- Is pre-authorization required?
    
- Are there annual limits?
    
- Are there exclusions?
    
- Does the therapist need to meet specific qualification requirements?
    
- Is reimbursement available for online sessions?
    

Always check the current terms of your specific policy rather than relying on a general statement about insurance.

# Is Therapy Worth the Money?

This is a personal decision.

Therapy can be a significant financial commitment, especially if sessions are weekly.

But it can also provide support for problems that affect:

- Work
    
- Education
    
- Relationships
    
- Sleep
    
- Concentration
    
- Emotional well-being
    
- Daily functioning
    

The value of therapy isn't simply whether you “feel better” after each session.

It can also involve learning skills that you continue using outside therapy.

For example:

**Therapy session → learn a skill → practice it during the week → handle situations differently → gradually improve functioning**

However, therapy isn't guaranteed to work equally well for everyone.

Treatment approach, therapist fit, consistency, the nature of the problem, and other circumstances can all affect outcomes.

# How Many Therapy Sessions Will You Need?

There is no universal number.

Some people may benefit from a relatively short course of therapy focused on a specific problem.

Others may need longer-term treatment.

Your needs may depend on:

- What you're experiencing
    
- How long you've been struggling
    
- Severity of symptoms
    
- Treatment approach
    
- Your goals
    
- Your progress
    
- Other circumstances
    

Be cautious about anyone who promises that a particular number of sessions will “cure” you.

A qualified therapist should be able to discuss treatment goals and review progress over time.

# How Much Could Therapy Cost Per Month?

Your monthly cost depends largely on how frequently you attend.

For example, if a therapist charges **₹1,000 per session**:

- 1 session/month = ₹1,000
    
- 2 sessions/month = ₹2,000
    
- 4 sessions/month = ₹4,000
    

At **₹2,000 per session**:

- 1 session/month = ₹2,000
    
- 2 sessions/month = ₹4,000
    
- 4 sessions/month = ₹8,000
    

At **₹3,000 per session**:

- 1 session/month = ₹3,000
    
- 2 sessions/month = ₹6,000
    
- 4 sessions/month = ₹12,000
    

These are simple examples, not recommendations for how frequently someone should attend therapy.

Your therapist should help determine an appropriate schedule based on your situation and treatment goals.

# What If I Can't Afford Therapy?

Cost should not automatically prevent you from seeking mental-health support.

There are several possibilities to explore.

## 1. Ask about lower-cost sessions

Some therapists offer reduced fees for students, people with financial difficulties, or clients who commit to a regular schedule.

This varies by professional.

You can simply ask:

> “Do you offer a sliding-scale or lower-cost fee?”

---

## 2. Look for community services

Hospitals, universities, NGOs, community organizations, and training clinics may offer lower-cost counselling or psychological services.

Availability varies by location.

---

## 3. Consider government services

India's **Tele-MANAS** program provides free 24/7 tele-mental-health support nationwide.

You can call:

**14416**

or

**1800-89-14416**

The Government of India's National Mental Health Programme describes Tele-MANAS as a 24/7 tele-mental-health service designed to provide accessible and affordable mental-health care across India.

The service can provide counselling and, when needed, connect people with higher levels of mental-health care.

---

## 4. Look at teaching or training clinics

Some psychology training institutions and clinics provide services at reduced rates.

If you consider this option, ask about the level of supervision and the qualifications of the person providing care.

---

## 5. Ask therapists about session frequency

If weekly therapy isn't financially realistic, discuss your options with the therapist.

Depending on the situation, they may be able to recommend an appropriate schedule.

Don't change a clinically recommended treatment schedule without discussing it with your provider.

# Free Mental-Health Support in India

For people who cannot afford private therapy, **Tele-MANAS is an important option**.

The Government of India's service provides free tele-mental-health support and is available 24/7. The public can access it at **14416** or **1800-89-14416**.

Tele-MANAS uses a tiered system. Counsellors provide initial support, and cases requiring more specialized care can be escalated to mental-health professionals or linked with appropriate services.

This can be particularly useful if:

- You aren't sure where to start
    
- Private therapy is too expensive
    
- You live in an area with limited mental-health services
    
- You want to talk to someone before deciding about ongoing therapy

# How to Choose a Therapist on a Budget

If you're working with a limited budget, don't automatically choose the cheapest option.

Instead, consider **value and suitability**.

Ask:

### Is the professional appropriately qualified?

Check their education, professional credentials, and relevant training.

### Do they work with your concern?

A therapist who regularly treats your specific concern may be more suitable than someone simply offering a lower fee.

### How long is each session?

A ₹1,000 session lasting 30 minutes isn't necessarily equivalent to a ₹1,500 session lasting 60 minutes.

### Do they offer online sessions?

This may provide more flexibility.

### Is the fee sustainable?

A slightly more expensive therapist whom you can consistently afford may be more practical than a very expensive therapist you can only attend once.

# Don't Choose a Therapist Solely by Price

A very low fee isn't necessarily a sign of poor-quality therapy.

Likewise, a very high fee isn't automatically a sign of superior treatment.

When choosing a therapist, consider the combination of:

**Qualifications + relevant experience + treatment approach + therapeutic fit + affordability**

The best therapist for you is not necessarily the most expensive one.

Fees depend on the individual professional and service rather than the label alone.

- Qualifications
    
- Scope of practice
    
- Experience
    
- Treatment approach
    
- Session length

# Is Online Therapy Cheaper Than In-Person Therapy?

Sometimes, but not always.

Online therapy can reduce certain overhead costs and may give you access to professionals outside your immediate area.

However, many therapists charge similar fees for online and in-person sessions.

The main advantage may sometimes be **convenience rather than price**.

Online therapy can eliminate:

- Travel time
    
- Transportation costs
    
- Waiting-room time
    

For some people, that makes therapy easier to maintain consistently.

# When Should You Not Let Cost Delay Getting Help?
