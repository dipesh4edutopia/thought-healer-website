If you're asking this because you're thinking about building a **mental-health AI chatbot/app or website**, the strongest content opportunities are usually questions phrased like:

> **“Why am I feeling ___?”**  
> **“Do I have ___?”**  
> **“How do I stop ___?”**  
> **“Is ___ normal?”**  
> **“How can I help someone with ___?”**

Those search patterns are particularly useful for building an FAQ/content strategy because they map directly to what users are trying to understand.

_Important: search popularity doesn't mean a person has a particular condition; mental-health symptoms overlap heavily, so an AI tool should avoid diagnosing users from a search/question alone._
